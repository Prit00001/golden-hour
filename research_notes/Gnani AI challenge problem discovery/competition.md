# Gnani AI "Great Indian AI (Internship) Challenge" (2026): Verified Competition Facts

Research date: 2026-10-30. Pages were fetched on this date. The official landing page (https://www.gnani.ai/internship) shows no publication date. Its countdown read "Submissions close in 28d…" when fetched, so the page was live and current. Press articles are dated 2026-10-01.

## 1. Objectives, vision, themes/tracks

### Takeaway
The theme is "Build Sovereign AI for India" on Gnani's own models. There are **no tracks and no fixed problem statement**: the brief is open, and any working application on at least one Gnani model or API that meets a practical need qualifies. The ten award categories work as de facto themes.

### Cited Findings
- Theme/tagline: "Build Sovereign AI for India." Participants build on Gnani AI's own models and compete for paid internships and awards. — [Gnani landing page](https://www.gnani.ai/internship)
- "The brief is open." There is no fixed problem statement. An entry is "a working application built on at least one Gnani AI model or API" that addresses a practical need. — [Gnani landing page](https://www.gnani.ai/internship)
- Example ideas on the official page, each mapping to an award: a Kannada farmer advisory helpline, a Hinglish kirana phone-order assistant, a noise-robust call summariser, and a Tamil quiz tutor. — [Gnani landing page](https://www.gnani.ai/internship)
- CTO Ananth Nagaraj: "Sovereign AI needs both foundational models and people who can turn them into useful applications." — [CXOToday, 2026-10-01](https://cxotoday.com/media-coverage/gnani-ai-launches-the-great-indian-ai-internship-challenge/)
- Press says the scope is not limited to voice. It also covers tools and workflows for language, information and enterprise tasks. — [CXOToday, 2026-10-01](https://cxotoday.com/media-coverage/gnani-ai-launches-the-great-indian-ai-internship-challenge/)

### Inferences
- With no tracks, the award categories (Section 7) are the best guide to what Gnani values: Indic and low-resource languages, code-mixing, noisy telephony audio, real-world usefulness, and creative model use.
- The challenge doubles as a recruiting funnel for interns, so a project that shows hiring-relevant skill with Gnani's stack is likely to help at interview.

### Gaps
- No separate vision or mission document for the challenge was found beyond the tagline and the CTO quote.

## 2. Eligibility, registration, participation

### Takeaway
The challenge is for students aged 18 or over, currently enrolled at a college or university in India, working solo or in a team of exactly two. Registration is through Gnani's own form, and the challenge is not listed on any external hackathon platform.

### Cited Findings
- Entrants must be currently enrolled in a degree or course at a college or university in India and be 18 or older on the day of registration. — [Gnani landing page](https://www.gnani.ai/internship)
- Entries are solo or a team of two, and both teammates must be eligible. Larger teams are not allowed. — [Gnani landing page](https://www.gnani.ai/internship); terms: "individually or in a team of up to two" — [Registration/terms page](https://www.gnani.ai/great-indian-ai-internship-registration-form)
- Each teammate accepts the terms individually and is interviewed and considered for an offer separately. — [Gnani landing page](https://www.gnani.ai/internship)
- Registration fields: name, email, phone, college, degree/course, discipline, year of study, graduation year, city, solo/team, and teammate email. For teams, credits and all contest communication go to the primary registrant. The teammate's email is "kept for verification and testing only." — [Gnani landing page](https://www.gnani.ai/internship); [Registration/terms page](https://www.gnani.ai/great-indian-ai-internship-registration-form)
- The page states that access for non-institutional email addresses was to open in the second week of October. — [Gnani landing page](https://www.gnani.ai/internship)
- One entry per participant or team. — [Gnani landing page](https://www.gnani.ai/internship)

### Inferences
- Students outside India, or enrolled at non-Indian institutions, appear ineligible.
- The rules don't explicitly say whether a person can register solo and also join a team, but "one entry per participant" implies they cannot.

### Gaps
- No Unstop, Devfolio, HackerEarth or Devpost listing was found. Searches returned only the official page and press.
- The rules don't say whether recent graduates, part-time or distance-learning students, or PhD students qualify, beyond "currently enrolled in a degree or course."

## 3. Submission requirements, timeline, stages

### Takeaway
A submission is a **working application** plus a **public demo video posted on LinkedIn and/or X** (with tags and hashtags), a written explanation, and a declaration of the Gnani models/APIs used. The deadline is **Nov 10, 2026, 11:59 PM IST, with no extensions**. There are no hackathon rounds. After the close come an engagement check, an eligibility check, shortlisting, interviews, and offers.

### Cited Findings
- Required submission content:
  1. Project title, problem solved, target users, the Gnani AI models/APIs used, and each member's contribution (editable until final submission).
  2. A public demo video of the working application, posted on LinkedIn and/or X with #GnaniAI and #GreatIndianAIInternshipChallenge, tagging Gnani AI.
  3. Nomination of those post(s) through the submission form, one link per platform. The post must already be live, and "tagging alone does not complete your entry."
  4. A written project explanation.
  — [Gnani landing page](https://www.gnani.ai/internship); [Registration/terms page](https://www.gnani.ai/great-indian-ai-internship-registration-form)
- The submission form link is "sent to your email" rather than posted publicly. — [Gnani landing page](https://www.gnani.ai/internship)
- No source mentions a code repository or a pitch deck as a requirement.
- Timeline (official page): contest starts and registration opens **Oct 1, 2026**, for a 41-day build window. Final submissions close **Nov 10, 2026, 11:59 PM IST**. Review and individual interviews for shortlisted participants follow, and six-month paid internships are scheduled to start **Jan 1, 2027**. — [Gnani landing page](https://www.gnani.ai/internship)
- **Start-date conflict:** Press says the challenge "opens September 30." — [CXOToday, 2026-10-01](https://cxotoday.com/media-coverage/gnani-ai-launches-the-great-indian-ai-internship-challenge/); [FinTech BizNews, 2026-10-01](https://fintechbiznews.com/fintech-technology/great-indian-ai-internship-challenge-by-gnani-ai). The official page says Oct 1. This doesn't matter now that the window is open.
- The terms say "by November 10, 2026 (final, no extensions)." — [Registration/terms page](https://www.gnani.ai/great-indian-ai-internship-registration-form)
- "Only engagement recorded by the published closing cutoff counts." — [Registration/terms page](https://www.gnani.ai/great-indian-ai-internship-registration-form)

### Inferences
- As of 2026-10-30, about **11 days remain**. Scope should be sized for a working demo that can be built in roughly a week, leaving time for the video to collect engagement before the cutoff.
- A public repo isn't required, but including one in the post or explanation probably helps in the "genuine use" check and at interview. This is an assumption.

### Gaps
- No video length limit is stated, but the "Demo Day Drop" award is for the best **60-second** demo.
- No published date for announcing interview shortlists or award winners.

## 4. Judging criteria, weights, process, jury

### Takeaway
**Internship shortlisting is engagement-first**: verified reactions, comments and reshares on the demo posts, then an eligibility check for genuine Gnani use, then interviews. **Awards are judged separately**, each on its category's criteria. No numeric weights or jury are published.

### Cited Findings
- Internship selection, in order:
  1. Engagement check (verified reactions, comments and reshares on nominated posts).
  2. Eligibility check (genuine use of Gnani AI technology and participation requirements).
  3. Shortlisting of the eligible projects with the strongest verified engagement.
  4. Individual interviews, including both teammates.
  5. Written offers based on assessed skills, interview performance and fit for available roles.
  — [Gnani landing page](https://www.gnani.ai/internship)
- "Awards are decided on each category's criteria, irrespective of internship selection." — [Gnani landing page](https://www.gnani.ai/internship)
- The terms refer to shortlisting "under the scoring method on the programme page", but no numeric scoring method or weights were found there. — [Registration/terms page](https://www.gnani.ai/great-indian-ai-internship-registration-form); [Gnani landing page](https://www.gnani.ai/internship)
- Prohibited: purchased engagement, bots, paid promotion, engagement exchanges, plagiarism and misleading demos. "Invalid interactions may be excluded, and entries with substantiated breaches may be disqualified." — [Registration/terms page](https://www.gnani.ai/great-indian-ai-internship-registration-form)
- Participation guarantees neither an interview nor an internship, and an internship does not guarantee employment. — [CXOToday, 2026-10-01](https://cxotoday.com/media-coverage/gnani-ai-launches-the-great-indian-ai-internship-challenge/)

### Inferences
- For the internship path, the shareability of the demo post matters most. A clear, relatable, India-specific use case with a short, punchy video is strategically important.
- For awards, a project can aim at one specific category. Since no team can win more than one award, there is little point in trying to qualify for several.

### Gaps
- No jury or judges are named.
- No weights or rubric within each award category are published.
- No definition of "genuine use of Gnani AI technology" is given, for example a minimum share of the pipeline that must run on Gnani.

## 5. Gnani technologies/APIs: required or optional; credits

### Takeaway
Entries **must** use and name at least one Gnani model or API. The officially listed options are **Prisma (STT)**, **Timbre (TTS, beta)** and **Evon v3.3 (open-weights LLM on Hugging Face)**. The API docs also expose **voice cloning** (`vachana-vc-v1`). Each registration gets **5,000 programme credits**. The agent builder is out of scope.

### Cited Findings
- The terms permit "Gnani AI's models and/or APIs": Prisma (speech-to-text), Timbre (text-to-speech, beta), and Evon (30B-parameter model on Hugging Face). — [Registration/terms page](https://www.gnani.ai/great-indian-ai-internship-registration-form)
- The landing page's build cards list **Prisma v2.5** (STT, "Trained on 14M+ hours of telephonic audio"), **Timbre v2.5** (TTS, Beta), and **Evon v3.3** (30B mixture-of-experts reasoning LLM, Apache 2.0 open weights on Hugging Face by request, "not yet on the API platform"). — [Gnani landing page](https://www.gnani.ai/internship)
- The same page's banner says: "Introducing our latest Speech-to-text Model - Prisma v3.0." Its build card still says v2.5. — [Gnani landing page](https://www.gnani.ai/internship)
- The agent builder is out of scope, and no APIs are offered for it. — [Gnani landing page](https://www.gnani.ai/internship)
- Each registration gets 5,000 programme credits. For teams, the credits go to the primary registrant's account. — [Gnani landing page](https://www.gnani.ai/internship); [Registration/terms page](https://www.gnani.ai/great-indian-ai-internship-registration-form)
- Access points: the API platform at https://app.gnani.ai/voice, docs at https://docs.gnani.ai, the Evon model at https://huggingface.co/gnani/gnani-evon-v3.3-30B-A3B, and a Discord support server at https://discord.gg/aECcvtABpq. — [Gnani landing page](https://www.gnani.ai/internship)
- API details from the docs:
  - **STT REST:** `POST https://api.vachana.ai/stt/v3`, models `prisma-v2.5` (default) and `prisma-v3.0`. Audio up to 30 s per request; WAV/MP3/OGG/FLAC/AAC/M4A; 8–44.1 kHz. `indic-auto` language detection works only on v3.0.
  - **STT realtime:** WebSocket `wss://api.vachana.ai/stt/v3/stream` (16-bit mono PCM, 8 or 16 kHz).
  - **Batch transcription:** documented separately.
  - **TTS:** REST, SSE and WebSocket. Model `timbre-v2.5` is recommended; v2.0 is "deprecated soon". Voices are chosen by name, e.g. "Nalini".
  - **Voice cloning:** an embeddings endpoint takes a 5–30 s reference clip, then synthesis uses model `vachana-vc-v1`.
  - **Auth:** `X-API-Key-ID` header.
  — [Gnani docs quick start](https://docs.gnani.ai/api/introduction/quick-start)
- Models page:
  - Prisma v2.5 supports 40+ languages, with diarization, code-switching and overlapping speech.
  - Timbre v2.5 supports 21+ languages, with under 100 ms time-to-first-audio.
  - Evon v3.3: about 3.5B active parameters per token, 128K context, tool orchestration, 11 Indian languages, single-node self-hostable.
  - **Warp v2.0**, a 5B speech-to-speech model with under 200 ms P95 latency, is listed, but its API availability isn't stated.
  — [Gnani models page](https://www.gnani.ai/models)

### Inferences
- The likely build path is Prisma STT and/or Timbre TTS through the API using the credits, with reasoning from Evon v3.3 self-hosted or another LLM (see Section 6). Evon has no API, so using it requires a GPU node. Running 30B mixture-of-experts weights is a heavy lift for a student.
- Voice cloning (`vachana-vc-v1`) is in the docs but **not named in the challenge rules**. Using it alongside Prisma or Timbre is probably fine as "Gnani AI technology", but that is not confirmed.
- The 30-second limit on REST STT means longer calls need the streaming or batch endpoints. This matters for call-summariser style projects.

### Gaps
- The programme terms say credit terms "explain what credits cover", but no credit-to-usage conversion was found (for example, minutes of STT or characters of TTS per credit).
- The names the user asked about: **Inya** appears only in a login URL (`/realms/inya/`) on the models page. **Vachana** appears only as the API domain and voice-clone model name. **Armour** was not found on the challenge, models or docs pages. None of these is named in the challenge rules.
- No confirmation that Warp or Artha can be used through the API in this challenge.

## 6. Restrictions on external models, frameworks, APIs

### Takeaway
**No explicit rule allows or bans** third-party LLMs, APIs or frameworks. The only firm requirements are genuine use of at least one Gnani model or API, naming the ones used, and a non-misleading demo.

### Cited Findings
- An entry must be "a working application built on at least one Gnani AI model or API" and must name "the Gnani AI models or APIs it uses." — [Gnani landing page](https://www.gnani.ai/internship)
- "Entries must show genuine use of Gnani AI technology and meet the participation requirements." — [Registration/terms page](https://www.gnani.ai/great-indian-ai-internship-registration-form)
- Misleading demos and plagiarism are prohibited. — [Registration/terms page](https://www.gnani.ai/great-indian-ai-internship-registration-form)
- Participants should use synthetic data, not real phone numbers, account numbers, Aadhaar, PAN or recorded calls. — [Gnani landing page](https://www.gnani.ai/internship)

### Inferences
- Combining Gnani STT/TTS with a third-party LLM appears permitted, but carries risk if a reviewer judges Gnani's role minor. To be safe, the core of the experience (speech in or out, Indic language handling) should visibly run on Gnani, and the explanation should state exactly which parts do.
- Replacing Gnani STT/TTS with competitors (e.g., other STT vendors) inside the same app would likely undercut the "genuine use" check.

### Gaps
- No rule found on IP ownership, licensing of submitted code, or open-sourcing.
- No published rule on pre-existing code or projects started before Oct 1. Only "plagiarism" is banned.

## 7. Prizes/awards and what each rewards

### Takeaway
There are two reward tracks: (a) up to **100 paid six-month internships**, with stipends of up to ₹1 lakh per month as a ceiling; (b) **ten ₹50,000 category awards** (₹5 lakh total), with at most one award per individual or team.

### Cited Findings
- Up to 100 paid internships of six months from Jan 1, 2027. The stipend is up to ₹1 lakh per intern per month, paid individually and not split. "₹1 lakh per month is the maximum potential stipend, not a standard or guaranteed amount." Only a written offer confirms an internship. — [Gnani landing page](https://www.gnani.ai/internship); [Registration/terms page](https://www.gnani.ai/great-indian-ai-internship-registration-form); [FinTech BizNews, 2026-10-01](https://fintechbiznews.com/fintech-technology/great-indian-ai-internship-challenge-by-gnani-ai)
- High-performing interns may be considered for full-time roles. — [CXOToday, 2026-10-01](https://cxotoday.com/media-coverage/gnani-ai-launches-the-great-indian-ai-internship-challenge/)
- Ten awards of ₹50,000 each — [Gnani landing page](https://www.gnani.ai/internship):

  | Award | What it rewards |
  |---|---|
  | Main Character | Best regional or low-resource Indian language build |
  | Mixed Vibes | Best code-mixed voice experience |
  | Real Deal | Most useful real-world problem solved |
  | Noise Canceller | Best performance on noisy telephonic audio |
  | Two-Person Army | Best team-of-two project |
  | Solo Ninja | Best solo project |
  | Jugaad Genius | Most creative use of Gnani models |
  | Small Town, Big Build | Best entry from a Tier 2/3 town |
  | Demo Day Drop | Best 60-second demo video |
  | Crowd Favourite | Public votes on LinkedIn and X using #GreatIndianAIInternshipChallenge & #GnaniAI |

- "No individual or team can win more than one award." "Purchased votes, bots and engagement exchanges don't count towards Crowd Favourite or any other award." — [Gnani landing page](https://www.gnani.ai/internship)
- CXOToday's launch article mentions no awards beyond internships. Awards appear on the official page. — [CXOToday, 2026-10-01](https://cxotoday.com/media-coverage/gnani-ai-launches-the-great-indian-ai-internship-challenge/)
- Gnani "may modify, suspend or cancel any part of this contest," including prizes. — [Registration/terms page](https://www.gnani.ai/great-indian-ai-internship-registration-form)

### Inferences
- The four example ideas on the official page line up with Main Character (Kannada and Tamil), Mixed Vibes (Hinglish), Noise Canceller (call summariser) and Real Deal. These are the technically oriented categories where Gnani's telephony-trained STT is the differentiator.
- How "Tier 2/3 town" is decided (college city vs. home city) is undefined. Registration collects "city."

### Gaps
- No tie-break rules and no announcement dates for awards.

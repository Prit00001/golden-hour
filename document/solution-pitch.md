# GOLDEN HOUR
### Your Voice-First AI Assistant for the Critical First Hour After Cyber Fraud

## Project Overview

Golden Hour is a multilingual, voice-first AI assistant designed to help victims of UPI, banking, and digital payment fraud take immediate action. In the critical first hour after a financial scam, victims often panic, struggle to understand reporting procedures, or simply do not know what steps to take. Golden Hour bridges this gap through calm, structured, step-by-step guidance in a language they understand.

## The Problem

India's digital payment ecosystem has grown rapidly, but awareness of cyber-fraud response procedures has not kept pace. Victims frequently lose valuable time figuring out whom to contact, what information to provide, and how to report incidents. Existing reporting channels can be difficult to navigate, particularly for people with limited digital literacy.

## Our Solution

Golden Hour acts as a personal AI-powered cyber-fraud response companion. Through a simple voice-based conversation, it understands the incident, collects relevant details, and guides victims through appropriate response procedures without overwhelming them with complicated instructions.

## Key Features

- **Multilingual Voice Assistance:** Makes cyber-fraud guidance accessible through natural voice interactions.
- **Guided Fraud Reporting:** Collects incident details through a structured conversational workflow.
- **Accurate Number Recognition:** Confirms critical information such as transaction amounts, phone numbers, and UTR/reference numbers.
- **Immediate Action Guidance:** Guides victims towards contacting the national cybercrime helpline (1930), their bank, and the official cybercrime reporting portal.
- **Automated Complaint Drafting:** Generates structured complaint drafts and bank emails using collected information.
- **Post-Incident Follow-ups:** Provides continued guidance and follow-up support.

## Technology Stack

- **Voice AI:** Gnani.ai Speech-to-Text and Text-to-Speech
- **Frontend:** React, TypeScript
- **Backend:** Python
- **AI Workflow:** Context-aware conversational processing and structured information collection

## What Makes Golden Hour Different?

Golden Hour is not just another cybercrime information portal or chatbot. It focuses on the victim's immediate emotional and practical needs. Its voice-first, multilingual approach reduces dependence on technical knowledge, lengthy forms, and English-language instructions. It connects victims with appropriate reporting channels while helping them prepare the information required to take action.

## Our Vision

To make the first hour after financial fraud less confusing, more accessible, and more actionable for every Indian citizen, regardless of their language or level of digital literacy.
